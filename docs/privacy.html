PRIVACY POLICY / LAST UPDATED NOVEMBER 13, 2026

Your frame.
Your privacy.

Vertical Mode adjusts the picture on your screen. Your viewing stays on your device.

What we collect

The YouTube Vertical Mode extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, trackers, or remote services. Its code and assets are included in the extension; it does not download or execute remote code.

What stays on your device

The extension saves two preferences using your browser's local extension storage: crop amount and horizontal framing position. These values are not sent to the developer or synchronized by the extension. Uninstalling the extension removes its local settings.

How the extension works

Content scripts run on the YouTube domains declared in the manifest: youtube.com, www.youtube.com, and www.youtube-nocookie.com. They interact with the video player, read video dimensions, and adjust its display locally. The popup checks the active tab so it can communicate with a supported player. This information is used only to operate the extension and is not recorded or transmitted by it.

Permissions

The only requested API permission is storage, which saves your framing preferences. YouTube site access allows the extension to add the Vertical mode button and adjust the player. The extension does not request access to unrelated websites.

Website and third parties

The Vertical Mode website contains no analytics or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and downloads are hosted on GitHub. Those services and YouTube may process information under their own privacy policies. Their operation is separate from the extension. If you contact the developer through GitHub, the information you choose to submit is handled by GitHub; public issues are visible to others.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the extension's behavior changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Questions can be submitted through the project's issue tracker ↗. Please keep personal information out of public issues.
