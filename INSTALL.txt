VERTICAL MODE / VERSION 1.1.0
A different perspective for your portrait monitor.

INSTALL IN CHROME OR DIA

1. Unzip the download into a folder you will keep.
2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome, or dia://extensions in Dia.
3. Turn on Developer mode.
4. Click Load unpacked.
5. Select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
   In a source checkout, select the extension folder instead.
6. Open or refresh a regular YouTube video.
7. Click Vertical mode beside the fullscreen control.

Move your browser to your portrait monitor before entering fullscreen.
Configure the monitor's portrait orientation in your operating system.

FIND YOUR FRAME

- Adjust framing: opens the panel at the top right of the player.
- Fill screen: fills the monitor while cropping the sides.
- Full video: shows the entire picture, with letterboxing when needed.
- Crop: blends between Full video and Fill screen without distortion.
- Horizontal framing: chooses which part of the picture stays visible.
- Reset framing: returns to a centered, filled screen.
- Escape, the X button, or Vertical mode again: exits the mode.
- Alt + Shift + V: toggles Vertical mode. Use Option on Mac.

On narrow players, the button shows a portrait-screen icon; its tooltip
and accessible label still say Vertical mode.

The extension popup enables the mode inside the window. To go directly
to fullscreen, use the button inside the YouTube player. If fullscreen
is blocked, you can also use YouTube's native fullscreen control.

UPDATE

Replace your extracted files with the new release. Click Reload in the
extensions manager, then refresh YouTube. Keep the extracted folder;
the browser loads the extension directly from it.

PRIVACY AND LIMITS

Only your crop and framing settings are saved locally. No account,
server, API key, analytics, or extension network requests are involved.
The picture is scaled and cropped, never stretched or rotated. The
extension does not follow people or remove black bars baked into a video.
Shorts and the miniplayer are outside this release's scope.
